a send-off, from one of your own
scroll
from Damola

Four years ago, I walked into EKSU not knowing a single face in this department. I'm walking out knowing all of you, and that's the part nobody really prepares you for. Not the exams, not the lecturers, not the projects that broke at 2am. It's everything in between that you end up remembering.

The 7am classes we showed up to looking like we'd been fighting for our lives since midnight. The notebooks of code we didn't understand, the borrowed handouts, the endless "guy, help me with this one" "abeg send the note" "who's got the past questions?"

And somehow, we still came. Sleepless nights, borrowed laptops, and enough faith to try again.

Then there were the results. Those three colours on the portal could change your entire mood before the page even finished loading. We failed things. We passed things. But we kept moving, and somewhere in all that complaining, we built friendships, the kind you could call at 2am for a note, a past question, or just someone to say "guy, I'm tired too." We debugged code together. I think we did more than that, we helped each other survive a season none of us fully understood while we were living through it.

I'm signing out this month. I won't pretend every moment was beautiful, it wasn't. But somehow, I made it through. We did.

So before I leave, I want to leave something behind that isn't just my story. Add your name. Drop a picture. Tell us something you remember. Let the log fill up, because someday, when we're all somewhere different, I want us to have somewhere to come back to and remember that before the jobs and the titles and whatever life becomes, we were just a bunch of students trying to make it through EKSU.

And somehow, we did.

— Lawal Oyindamola David (Damola)
